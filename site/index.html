The school

Practical training for the job you actually want.

Ten months, in one room, doing the work — not watching slides about it.

Rockview I.T. is a private IT skills school opening in Northdale, Pietermaritzburg in February 2027. We teach the practical side of IT: setting up networks, defending them, running cloud services and writing software.

You choose one of three career paths and stay on it for ten months. Every path ends in recognised vendor certifications, and every week ends with something you can do that you could not do on Monday.

This is a registered company with accreditation in place body and number to confirm. Ask us for the paperwork before you pay anything.

Questions

The things people ask first.

Plain answers, including the ones that are still being confirmed.

Ask us anything else on WhatsApp.

Do I need a laptop?

Yes. This is a bring-your-own-laptop programme and you will use it every day. Windows or Mac is fine. If you are buying one, 8 GB of RAM is the comfortable minimum. Tell us what you have when you apply and we will tell you honestly whether it will carry you through.

Do I need maths?

No degree, and no IT subjects at school, are needed to apply — this school is for matric school-leavers. You will do basic arithmetic (subnetting, storage sizes, simple logic) and we teach that from scratch.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Every path is priced per month over ten months, and your certification exam fees are covered by the school. Debit order, EFT and deposit options are being confirmed payment terms to confirm — ask for the current terms before you register.

Do I pay extra for exams?

Your certification exam fees are covered by the school.

No. We cover the exam fees for the certifications in your career path. [Add your retake policy here.]

What if I fail an exam?

Vendor exams can be retaken, and a failed attempt does not remove you from the programme. The retake policy and any extra voucher cost are to confirm; we will give it to you in writing at registration, not after.

Can I switch paths?

Ask before you register if you are unsure — that is the easy moment to change. Once the class has started, switching depends on space and on how far the group has gone. We will always tell you what is possible rather than promising it.
